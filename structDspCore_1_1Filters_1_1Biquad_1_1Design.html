Detailed Description

template<typename CoefficientType = float>
struct DspCore::Filters::Biquad::Design< CoefficientType >

Explicit-precision biquad coefficient designer.

Biquad design is stateless: it has no stream SampleType or recurrence StateType. CoefficientType alone controls all design arithmetic and defaults to float. The namespace-level functions remain a deduction-friendly shortcut.
