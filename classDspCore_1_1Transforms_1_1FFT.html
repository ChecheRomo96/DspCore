Detailed Description

template<typename Real = float>
class DspCore::Transforms::FFT< Real >

Configured radix-2 Cooley-Tukey FFT with forward and inverse operations.

Configure accepts only nonzero powers of two and allocates one workspace. Forward and Inverse are allocation-free thereafter and support identical input/output storage. Forward is unnormalised; Inverse applies 1/N.
