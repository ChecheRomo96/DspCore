Detailed Description

Configure DspCore against a matching Foundation package.

DspCore locates or fetches Foundation automatically (see Dependencies):

./scripts/configure.sh macos_arm64 --fresh
./scripts/build.sh macos_arm64 --config Debug

To use a specific Foundation package, point DspCore at its prefix:

./scripts/configure.sh macos_arm64 --fresh -- \
-DDSPCORE_FOUNDATION_PREFIX=../Foundation/dist/macos_arm64
