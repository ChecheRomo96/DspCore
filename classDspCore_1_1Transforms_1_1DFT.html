Detailed Description

template<typename Real = float>
class DspCore::Transforms::DFT< Real >

Configured direct discrete Fourier transform with forward and inverse operations.

Configure allocates one caller-invisible workspace. Forward and Inverse then perform no allocation, accept equal-sized configured blocks and permit exact in-place operation. Forward is unnormalised; Inverse divides by the configured size, so Inverse(Forward(x)) reconstructs x subject to floating-point rounding.
