Architecture and planned content

Dependency direction: Foundation -> DspCore -> MIDILAR. General utilities belong to Foundation, music theory to MCC, and MIDI interpretation to MIDILAR. DspCore processes signals independently.

Content (Core/version, LUT storage, interpolation, generators, single-rate filters, analysis measurements and processing interfaces are implemented; dynamics, broader filter families, response analysis and transforms are planned):

  • Numeric: scalar precision, ranges and explicit conversion policy.
  • LUT: 1D, 2D and 3D storage, independent of generators.
  • Interpolation: linear, bilinear and trilinear.
  • Generators: periodic tables, noise, envelopes, shaping and windows.
  • Filters: single-rate FIR/IIR processing and Biquad coefficient design; SOS, multirate and MIMO filter families are planned.
  • Dynamics: compressor, limiter and gate boundaries are planned.
  • Analysis: peak, RMS and envelope followers are implemented; frequency response, pole-zero and stability analysis are planned.
  • Processing: typed, non-owning fixed-size sample-block views for algorithms.
  • Transforms: DFT, IDFT, FFT and IFFT are planned.

MIDILAR's musical control layer interprets notes, pedals, RPN/NRPN, controllers and parameter bindings. It may consume DspCore signal processing, but DspCore never interprets MIDI messages.

Historical implementations were reviewed at MIDILAR commit bc70ce639f8bb72f79995e1ec742b73aef544112. They are design input, not a compatibility promise. See docs/Design/LegacyDspReview.md in the source checkout for findings and probe evidence.

Numeric and buffer contracts defines the numerical and buffer requirements for migration. Further implementation follows ACTION_PLAN.md phase by phase.

DspCore deliberately does not own data streams. A future runtime library will provide audio, MIDI, envelope and event transport, FIFOs, clocks, routing, MIMO graphs and adapters. That layer will pass caller-owned memory to DspCore algorithms through Processing::SampleBlock at its boundary.
