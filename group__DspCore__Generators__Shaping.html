Detailed Description

Atan/tanh raw, normalized and positive-half curves; gamma and gamma morph.

Atan/Tanh sample x in [-1,1]. Half variants sample [0,1]. Norm divides by atan(drive) or tanh(drive), giving exact endpoints after scaling. A singleton samples x=0 for every shaping family. Tiny positive normalized drive uses its linear limit. Raw atan may exceed magnitude one (bounded by pi/2); its scale must be <= maximum representable value / (pi/2).

Gamma samples x^gamma on [0,1] with endpoints zero and scale. GammaMorph uses exponent=pow(gain,morph); non-finite or zero derived exponents fail before writing. Positive finite gain and finite morph are required. Normalized results are bounded against numerical overshoot before scaling. This phase provides offline curve builders, not a per-sample waveshaper.
