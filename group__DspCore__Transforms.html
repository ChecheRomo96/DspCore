Detailed Description

Configured block Fourier transforms and their inverse operations.

DFT<Real> is the O(N^2) direct reference transform and accepts every nonzero configured size. FFT<Real> is an O(N log N) radix-2 transform and accepts only powers of two. Both consume caller-owned Processing::SampleBlock views of Foundation::Math::Complex<Real>, offer Forward and Inverse, and include ForwardReal for real input. Forward is unnormalised; Inverse applies 1/N, so a successful inverse of a successful forward transform reconstructs the input within floating-point error.

Configure allocates the transform workspace. Calls after Configure perform no allocation, require valid input/output views exactly N elements long, validate finite values and leave the output unchanged on failure. The workspace makes exact input/output aliasing safe. These are block transforms: they do not own a clock, stream, FIFO, hop size, window, or overlap-add policy. A streaming analysis/synthesis adaptor is a separate future API.
